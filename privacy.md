# Privacy Policy for MIDItoOSC

_Last updated: 2026-03-23_

## Overview

MIDItoOSC is designed to run locally on your Mac.  
The app processes MIDI input and displays OSC equivalent commands.

## Data collection

MIDItoOSC does **not** collect personal data from users.

- No account creation
- No analytics SDK
- No advertising SDK
- No tracking

## Data transmission

MIDItoOSC does not transmit personal data to external servers by default.

Any MIDI/OSC content shown in the app is generated from your local session and devices.

## Data storage

MIDItoOSC does not maintain a remote database of user data.  
Local app state (such as UI preferences) may be stored on-device by macOS.

## Third-party services

MIDItoOSC does not rely on third-party analytics or ad networks.

## Children’s privacy

MIDItoOSC is a technical utility app and is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated over time.  
Any updates will be reflected on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

## Contact

If you have privacy-related questions, contact the app publisher through the support channel provided for MIDItoOSC.
